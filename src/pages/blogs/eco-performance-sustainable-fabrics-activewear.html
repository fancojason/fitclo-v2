Certifications Matter: GRS & OEKO-TEX

When you market a "Sustainable" collection, your customers need proof. We provide full traceability for our eco-fabrics, including:

GRS

Global Recycled Standard

Verifies recycled content and monitors social/environmental practices in production.

100

OEKO-TEX® Standard 100

Certifies that the fabric is free from harmful chemicals and safe for human skin.

Can Eco-Fabrics Match 230GSM Interlock?

The short answer: **Yes.** Modern recycled Nylon-66 provides the same tensile strength and "hand-feel" as virgin nylon. In blind tests, professional athletes cannot distinguish the performance difference between FITCLO's standard interlock and our recycled variations.
