Why Choose Embroidery for Your Lifestyle Collection?

1. Superior Durability

Unlike prints that may crack or fade over time, embroidery threads are woven directly into the fabric, ensuring the logo lasts for the entire lifespan of the garment.

2. Premium 3D Texture

Embroidery provides a tactile, elevated feel. Whether it's a flat stitch or a 3D puff embroidery, it adds a level of sophistication that customers associate with high-end luxury brands.

Optimized for Hoodies & Sweatpants

Embroidery is particularly effective on heavier fabrics. For our 300-400GSM cotton-blend fleece hoodies and joggers, embroidery provides the necessary weight and structure to prevent the fabric from puckering around the logo area.

  • Small Details: Our multi-head machines can handle intricate designs with up to 12 different thread colors.
  • Positioning: Popular choices include the left chest, center chest, sleeve cuffs, or the hip area of sweatpants.
  • Thread Options: We offer high-sheen polyester threads, matte cotton threads, and even metallic options for a truly "Luxopack" feel.
