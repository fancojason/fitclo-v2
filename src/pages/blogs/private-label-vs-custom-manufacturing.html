Private Label vs. Custom Manufacturing: Choosing Your Path to Success

BUSINESS STRATEGY • June 28, 2026

When you decide to launch or scale an activewear brand, you'll encounter two primary manufacturing models: **Private Label** (Ready-to-Ship/Customized) and **Custom Manufacturing** (OEM/Full Custom). Choosing the wrong one can lead to wasted capital or missed market opportunities.

1. Private Label (The Speed King)

Using our pre-designed, high-performance patterns and adding your branding (Logo, Tags, Packaging).

  • MOQ: Low (Starts at 50 pcs)
  • Lead Time: Fast (7-15 days)
  • Best For: Testing new markets, influencers, and brands that want to launch quickly with proven styles.

2. Custom Manufacturing (The Visionary)

Creating a unique product from scratch based on your tech packs, custom measurements, and exclusive fabric blends.

  • MOQ: Higher (Typically 300+ pcs per color)
  • Lead Time: Longer (35-60 days)
  • Best For: Established brands with a unique design DNA that want total market exclusivity.

Which Model is Right for You?

Most successful brands follow a hybrid approach. At FITCLO, we recommend starting with **Private Label** for your core essentials (Leggings, Bras) to build immediate cash flow, while simultaneously developing 1-2 **Full Custom** "hero" pieces to establish your unique brand identity.

!

**Budget Check:** Custom manufacturing requires significant investment in sampling and pattern making. Private label eliminates these upfront costs.

!

**Market Speed:** If you see a trending color or silhouette on TikTok, Private Label is the only way to capitalize on it before the trend fades.
