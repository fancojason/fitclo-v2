Choosing the Right Shipping Method

1. Air Express (DHL/FedEx/UPS)

**Best for:** Samples and small MOQ orders (50-200 pcs).
**Transit Time:** 3-5 business days.
**Advantage:** Speed and door-to-door convenience.

2. Air Freight (Airport-to-Airport)

**Best for:** Bulk orders over 300kg.
**Transit Time:** 7-10 business days.
**Advantage:** Balanced cost-to-speed ratio for large volume seasonal launches.

3. Sea Freight (LCL/FCL)

**Best for:** Large stock replenishments and low-urgency bulk.
**Transit Time:** 25-45 days (depending on destination).
**Advantage:** Lowest cost per unit, essential for scaling wholesale margins.

Navigating Customs & Duties

One of the biggest hurdles for new importers is the complexity of HS Codes and import duties. Activewear typically falls under specific textile categories that vary by fiber content (Nylon vs. Polyester). We provide:

  • Accurate Commercial Invoices and Packing Lists.
  • Certificate of Origin (CO) if required for duty-free entry in specific regions.
  • DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) services for the US and EU, where we handle all taxes and customs clearance for you.
