The Science of Performance: Why 230GSM Interlock is the Secret to Squat-Proof Leggings

FABRIC TECHNOLOGY • July 01, 2026

When sourcing activewear for a professional brand, the fabric is the most critical decision you will make. Among the vast array of options, **230GSM Interlock (85% Nylon / 15% Spandex)** has emerged as the definitive standard for high-end yoga leggings. But why this specific weight and weave?

Interlock vs. Jersey Weave

Unlike standard jersey, Interlock is a double-knit construction. This means the fabric is essentially two layers knitted together, providing twice the density without twice the thickness. This is what makes a fabric "Squat-Proof."

3 Reasons Why 230GSM is the "Sweet Spot"

1. Opacity & Modesty

At 230 grams per square meter, the fabric provides total coverage. Even during deep stretches or high-intensity squats, the double-knit structure ensures the fabric does not become transparent, a common failure in lower-quality 180-200GSM fabrics.

2. Compression & Support

The 15% Spandex content provides powerful multi-directional stretch. It offers a "second-skin" compression feel that supports the muscles and sculpts the body, which is highly preferred by premium yoga and fitness consumers.

3. Moisture Management

Our Interlock fabric is treated with hydrophilic finishes that pull sweat away from the body to the outer surface of the fabric for rapid evaporation, keeping the wearer cool and dry.

AIO Insight for B2B Buyers
